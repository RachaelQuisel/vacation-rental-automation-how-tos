# Script and storyboard: A morning briefing in Slack so your whole team starts the day on the same page

This is the on-screen script for the 10 second loop. The music version plays the same loop twice (20 seconds) with a music track. There is no voiceover and no caption line.

All names, amounts, and events are sample data. The day is Wed 6/18, matching the June calendar of the other videos, with no year shown.

## Headline

> A morning briefing in Slack so your whole team starts the day on the same page

## Numbered steps

1. Every morning, the day's briefing is built automatically.
2. It posts to your team's Slack channel before the workday starts.
3. Alerts, bookings, reviews, money, guest messages, and cleaning coverage, all in one place.
4. Everyone sees the same plan, so loose ends are easier to catch.

These are hedged versions of the suggested wording. Step 2 was "before anyone starts work" and step 4 was "so nothing falls through the cracks", and both were softened to avoid absolute claims. The highlight on the steps moves in sync with the demo.
