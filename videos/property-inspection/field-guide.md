# Field guide: When an inspection finds a problem, the right contractor is scheduled automatically

This guide explains the short property inspection video (the 10-second loop and its 20-second music version). It covers what each label on screen means, what the inspector and the contractor each receive, and some common questions.

Everything in the video is **sample data**. Nina L. (inspector), Marco R. (plumber) and Leo S. (spa tech) are invented names. The dates, times and messages are examples. "La Maison" appears as the sender name for the property.

## What the video shows

**Headline:** "When an inspection finds a problem, the right contractor is scheduled automatically"

**The four steps**, which light up as the demo reaches each one:

1. Your inspector works through every task on a checklist app.
2. Spot a problem? One tap texts the right contractor, like your plumber, spa tech, landscaper, or electrician.
3. The contractor gets the details and picks the next open time.
4. Everyone gets a confirmation by text and email, and your maintenance channel in Slack is updated.

## 2. What each person receives (sample wording from the video)

### The inspector (Nina L.)

- **In the checklist app:** they tick tasks, flag a problem with a short note and a photo, pick the contractor type, and tap **Send to plumber**. The button confirms **Texted Marco R. ✓**, and the flagged item shows **Plumber texted ✓**.
- **Text, once a time is picked:** "Plumber confirmed: Marco R., Thu 6/19 at 1 PM"
- **Text confirmation** (as on the card): "Confirmed: plumber visit Thu 6/19 at 1 PM for the kitchen sink leak."
- **Email:** subject "Plumber visit confirmed: Thu 6/19, 1 PM", starting "Leak under the kitchen sink, found on Wednesday's inspection."

The video shows two text moments for the inspector: the banner and the text card. They may be the same message shown two ways. What actually arrives depends on how the owner has set things up.

### The contractor (Marco R.)

- **Job text:** "New job at La Maison: leak under the kitchen sink, found on inspection (photo attached). Pick a time:" followed by the open times (Thu 6/19 · 1 PM, marked Next open; Fri 6/20 · 9 AM; Fri 6/20 · 2 PM).
- **Their reply:** they tap a time, and it's sent back ("Thu 6/19 · 1 PM").
- **Confirmation text:** "You're booked: Thu 6/19 at 1 PM. Details are in your email." The card version reads "Confirmed: plumber visit Thu 6/19 at 1 PM for the kitchen sink leak."
- **Email:** subject "Plumber visit confirmed: Thu 6/19, 1 PM", starting "Leak under the kitchen sink, found on Wednesday's inspection."

### The team (#maintenance channel)

- **Post from the La Maison app:** "Plumber scheduled from today's inspection", with a card reading "Leak under the kitchen sink · Marco R. (plumber) · Thu 6/19, 1 PM · found by Nina L."

## 3. FAQ

**For inspectors**

**Do I need to call or text the contractor myself?**
In the flow shown, no. Flagging the item and tapping the contractor button sends the text. You may still want to reach out directly for anything urgent or unusual.

**What if I pick the wrong contractor type?**
Let the owner or property manager know as soon as you can so they can redirect the job. How a correction works depends on their setup.

**Can I flag more than one problem on an inspection?**
The video shows one flagged item. A real checklist could include several, each sent to the matching contractor type, depending on how it's configured.

**Do I have to attach a photo?**
The video shows a photo with the flag because it helps the contractor understand the issue. Whether a photo is required is up to the owner.

**What if something is urgent, like an active leak?**
The scheduling texts are for booking a visit. For anything urgent or unsafe, follow the owner's emergency instructions and contact them directly.

**For contractors**

**Do I need to install an app?**
In the flow shown, the contractor only gets texts and an email; the checklist app is used by the inspector. Depending on the setup, you may just tap a time in the text.

**What if none of the offered times work for me?**
Reply to the text or contact the owner or property manager so they can offer other options. How other times are handled depends on their setup.

**What does "Next open" mean?**
It marks the earliest of the offered times. You don't have to take it. The other times listed are also available to pick.

**What if I book a time and then can't make it?**
Let the owner or property manager know as early as you can. They can usually reschedule or reassign the job; the exact steps depend on their process.

**Where are the job details?**
The text includes a short description of the issue and notes that a photo is attached. The confirmation email may include more details.

**Will I get a reminder before the visit?**
The video shows only the job text and the confirmations. Reminders may or may not be set up, so check with the owner.

**For everyone**

**Who sees the #maintenance post?**
Usually the people the owner has added to the team's maintenance channel, such as the owner and property managers. Contractors and inspectors may or may not be in that channel, depending on how the team is set up.

**Who gets the confirmation text and email?**
In the video, both the contractor and the inspector get the confirmation by text and email.

**Is the information in the video real?**
No. The "Sample data" tag marks it as invented. The names, dates, times, messages and the checklist are examples only.

**Is the chat app in the video Slack?**
The step text mentions Slack, and the chat window is styled like a team chat app. No logos are shown, and the post content is sample wording.
