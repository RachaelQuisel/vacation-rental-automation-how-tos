# Script and storyboard: When an inspection finds a problem, the right contractor is scheduled automatically

This is the on-screen script for the 10 second loop. The music version plays the same loop twice (20 seconds) with a music track. There is no voiceover and no caption line.

All names, dates, times, and messages are sample data. The names Nina L. (inspector), Marco R. (plumber), and Leo S. (spa tech) are invented. The month is a June that starts on a Sunday, with no year shown.

## Headline

> When an inspection finds a problem, the right contractor is scheduled automatically

## Numbered steps

1. Your inspector works through every task on a checklist app.
2. Spot a problem? One tap texts the right contractor, like your plumber, spa tech, landscaper, or electrician.
3. The contractor gets the details and picks the next open time.
4. Everyone gets a confirmation by text and email, and your maintenance channel in Slack is updated.

The steps are shown in Rachael's wording. The highlight on the steps moves in sync with the demo.
