# Field guide: Schedule your cleaners by text

This guide explains every label in the video, how the cleaner and the host each see the system, and the questions they're likely to ask. It's based on `script.md`, `../../src/v1.html`, and the other docs in this repo. Everything shown is sample data. The video shows how the system is meant to work. Where the real system's behavior isn't shown or written down, this guide says To confirm with Rachael.

## How each person sees it

**The cleaner**
1. They get a text from the host's business name with a short link. No app is needed.
2. They tap the link and see a page called "Pick your dates" with their first name and the open turnovers.
3. They tap the turnovers they want, then tap "Sign me up".
4. They're signed up, and the turnovers show their name on the shared calendar.
- Whether the cleaner also gets a confirmation text or a calendar invite after signing up isn't shown in this video. To confirm with Rachael. (The backup cleaner video shows calendar invites for turnovers.)
- How a cleaner cancels a turnover they signed up for is To confirm with Rachael.

**The host**
- The host sees the shared calendar fill in as cleaners sign up. "Open" shows which turnovers still need someone.
- Who gets the text and when it goes out (for example, all cleaners at once or one at a time) is To confirm with Rachael.

## FAQ

**Cleaner: Do I need to download an app?**
No app is shown. The invitation is a regular text, and the link opens a web page.

**Cleaner: What if two cleaners pick the same turnover?**
The video doesn't show this case. To confirm with Rachael.

**Cleaner: Can I pick more than one date?**
Yes. In the video, Maria picks two of the four open turnovers before tapping "Sign me up".

**Cleaner: What are the times next to each date?**
The cleaning window for that turnover, for example 11am to 3pm.

**Cleaner: What happens after I tap "Sign me up"?**
Your name goes on the shared calendar for the dates you picked. Whether you also get a confirmation is To confirm with Rachael.

**Host: How do I know which turnovers are still open?**
They show as "Open" on the shared calendar.

**Host: Is the link in the video real?**
No. "lmsb.co/pick" and all the names and dates are made-up sample data.
