# Script and storyboard: Schedule your cleaners by text

This is the on-screen script for the 10 second loop. The music version plays the same loop twice (20 seconds) with a music track. There is no voiceover and no caption line.

All names, dates, and links are sample data.

## Headline

> The smarter way to schedule your contractors

## Numbered steps

1. Your cleaner gets a text
2. They pick the dates they want to clean
3. They're signed up on your shared calendar

The highlight on the steps moves in sync with the phone demo.

## Phone screens

**Text thread**

- Sender: "La Maison Turnovers" (avatar "LM"), labeled "SMS"
- Message: "New turnovers are open at La Maison. Tap to pick your dates: lmsb.co/pick"
- Time label: "Now"

**Pick your dates**

- Title: "Pick your dates"
- Line under it: "Hi Maria, choose the turnovers you'd like."
- Four options (the sample June starts on a Sunday, so the weekdays line up):
  - Sat 6/14, 11am to 3pm
  - Tue 6/17, 10am to 2pm
  - Fri 6/20, 11am to 3pm
  - Sun 6/22, 12pm to 4pm
- Button: "Sign me up"

**Shared calendar**

- Title: "Shared calendar"
- Line under it: "Maria G. signed up for 2 turnovers"
- Month grid for 6/8 to 6/28. "Maria" on 6/14 and 6/20. "Open" on 6/17 and 6/22.
- Two entries under the grid: "Turnover · Maria G." for Sat 6/14 and Fri 6/20, 11am to 3pm.

**Paper note** (handwritten style, taped on)

> On the shared calendar ✓

## Beats

| Time (s) | Step shown | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 to 0.6 | 1 | Empty text thread on the phone. |
| 0.6 to 1.05 | 1 | The text message pops in. "Now" fades in at 0.9 to 1.2. |
| 1.7 | 1 | A tap ripple lands on the link. |
| 2.2 to 2.7 | 1 to 2 | "Pick your dates" slides in from the right. The highlight moves to step 2 at 2.3 to 2.6. |
| 3.15 | 2 | Tap on Sat 6/14. It shows as checked at 3.3. |
| 3.95 | 2 | Tap on Fri 6/20. It shows as checked at 4.1. |
| 4.75 to 5.1 | 2 | Tap on "Sign me up". The button presses in. |
| 5.25 to 5.75 | 2 to 3 | "Shared calendar" slides up. The highlight moves to step 3 at 5.35 to 5.65. |
| 6.0 | 3 | "Maria" pops onto 6/14, and its entry slides in below the grid. |
| 6.45 | 3 | "Maria" pops onto 6/20, and its entry slides in below the grid. |
| 6.9 to 7.35 | 3 | The paper note pops in. |
| 8.0 | 3 | Poster frame. Finished calendar, step 3, and the note are all on screen. |
| 9.0 to 9.6 | 3 to 1 | A blank text thread fades in over the phone. The note fades out. The highlight returns to step 1 at 9.1 to 9.5. |
| 10.0 | 1 | Loop point. The last frame matches the first frame. |

Background motion runs the whole time: four clouds drift right, and nine leaves and petals drift across the scene. Both wrap off screen, so the loop should look seamless.

## Audio (music version only)

- Excerpt: 76.95 s to 96.95 s of the track, chosen to start on a bar downbeat.
