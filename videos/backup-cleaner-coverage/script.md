# Script and storyboard: When your cleaner can't make it, the next one is asked automatically

This is the on-screen script for the 10 second loop. The music version plays the same loop twice (20 seconds) with a music track. There is no voiceover and no caption line.

All names, dates, and messages are sample data.

## Headline

> When your cleaner can't make it, the next one is asked automatically

## Numbered steps

1. Each booking creates a cleaning task
2. Your cleaner gets a calendar invite
3. If they decline, it goes to the next cleaner
4. You get a text if a day still needs coverage

The highlight on the steps moves in sync with the demo.

## Demo surfaces

**Painted room card**

- A guest room in the same painted style: a bed, a window with a sea view, a painting, a rug, a nightstand, and an arched front door.
- Two painted guests with suitcases stand in the doorway.
- Tag: "Thu 6/19 · 4 PM check-in". After the room is cleaned it reads "Ready for 4 PM check-in ✓".
- At the start the room is messy (rumpled duvet, towels on the floor, plates, a bottle, a shirt). Later a sweeping band with sparkles swaps in the clean room (made bed, folded towels, a plant).

**Tablet: Cleaning calendar** (tags "June 15 – 21" and "Sample data")

- The week Sun 6/15 to Sat 6/21. The sample June starts on a Sunday, so the weekdays line up.
- A "Stays" row with stay bars ("Stay, 2 nights") and a "Turnovers" row with four turnover chips at 11 AM to 3 PM.
- Sun 6/15: Maria G., Accepted.
- Tue 6/17: Rosa P., Accepted.
- Thu 6/19: New task, then Maria G. Invite sent, then Declined (name struck through), then Rosa P. Invite sent, then Accepted ✓.
- Sat 6/21: New task, then Asking cleaners, then Needs a cleaner (dashed outline and a "!" badge).
- Footer: "No turnovers yet this week", then "N cleaning tasks created", then "4 turnovers · 2 invites waiting on a reply", then "4 turnovers · 3 have a cleaner · Sat still open".

**Phone**

- "Maria's phone": a calendar invite card. "Calendar invite · Turnover at La Maison · Thu, Jun 19 · 11 AM – 3 PM · From: cleaning schedule", with Decline and Accept buttons. Maria taps Decline, and a "Declined" stamp lands.
- "Rosa's phone": the same invite. Rosa taps Accept, and an "Accepted ✓" stamp lands.
- "Your phone": a text from "Alerts" (bell icon, no phone number shown), "Today 9:30 AM": "Heads up: Sat 6/21 turnover still needs a cleaner."

**Paper note** (handwritten style, taped on)

> Declined invite, rerouted to Rosa ✓
