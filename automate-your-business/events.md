## Topics

### 01. The Loop Audit
Stop automating tasks, start automating loops.

- Map one recurring work loop: trigger, steps, decision, output, and escape hatch. Identify the single step you can build this week.
- 60 minutes, with about 22 of them hands-on.
- Take-home: your Loop Audit worksheet.

### 02. The Colleague Audit
Stop chatting with AI, start delegating to it.

- Choose a real task, write a checkable definition of done, and identify exactly the access an AI colleague needs to complete it. Focus on repeatable completion and a supervised filter before independent action.
- 60 minutes, with about 20 of them hands-on.
- Take-home: your Colleague Audit worksheet.

### 03. The Proof Audit
Stop buying AI tools, start proving they work.

- Judge whether AI is paying off by the speed of real outcomes. Draft a next-quarter budget split, a one-page Rulebook defining five contested business words, and one hard rule.
- 60 minutes, with about 20 of them hands-on.
- Take-home: your one-page Rulebook.
