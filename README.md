# Vacation Rental Automation How-tos

Short how-to videos that show automations for running a vacation rental, such as cleaner scheduling, tax prep, and cleaning coverage.

Videos are added through pull requests.

## Videos

- [Schedule your cleaners by text](videos/schedule-cleaners-by-text/): your cleaner gets a text, picks the dates they want to clean, and is signed up on your shared calendar.

More videos are planned. They will be added here as they are finished.

All videos use sample data. The names, dates, numbers, and links on screen are made up.

## How these videos are made

- [How these videos are made](docs/how-to-make-these-videos.md): the recipe, from the painted scene to the final export.
- [Design schema](docs/design-schema.md): palette, fonts, layout, filters, timeline, and file names.
- [Workflow diagrams](docs/workflow.md): the production pipeline and a beat-by-beat view of a loop.
- [Build code](src/): the scene file and scripts for the cleaner video.
